# Missing-Cards Catalog v2 — Post-Resync Authoring To-Do

*Generated against the current corpus: `data/cards.js`, 283 cards, 33% US (92 by place) / 191 non-US, 27 threads, 24 countries, span 105–2024. Every gap below was re-checked by name/person grep against the live data — nothing here is a false-gap carried over from the old list.*

## 1. Status

The vault resync did most of the old brief's work. Of the 92 distinct cards proposed in the pre-sync 180-card catalog (`docs/missing-cards.md`), **79 are now present and 13 are still missing — roughly 86% filled.** The entire A.1 Top-12 landed, along with the Next-Tier set (Gamma Knife, FT-NMR, Cyclades/Datagram, Long March, PSLV, Raman, Venetian Arsenal), and every promised "first-time country" arrived except one: **Austria** (Basic Oxygen Steelmaking), **Australia** (PERC), and **Denmark** (Gedser) are all now carded; **Hungary is the single promised country that never materialized** (Erdős–Rényi still absent — Hungary has zero cards in the 283). Where the corpus still leans, it leans in two directions. First, **modern East-Asian hardest-making is thin relative to its real-world weight**: South Korea holds only 3 cards, Taiwan only 3 (all TSMC/Hsinchu), Shenzhen only 1, and the Energy thread has *zero* China cards despite China making ~80% of the world's solar modules. Second, two thread panels are internally lopsided — **Electricity & Batteries** (6 cards, 0% US) jumps straight from Faraday 1831 to the 1991 Tokyo Li-ion cell with the entire lead-acid→NiCd→LiCoO₂ century blank, and **Software Abstraction**'s pre-1948 British/German foundations of computation (Turing, Boole, Lovelace, Leibniz) are missing before an all-US mid-century column. A few threads are already richly global and should be left alone (Section 4). Two data deltas the author should note from the resync: Nikon (1980) and Canon (1984) stepper *years are swapped* versus the old proposal, and the two proposed Zeiss optics cards collapsed into a single 2017 "Zeiss Lithography Optics."

## 3. Single-card foreign "turns" to convert into real tenures

These are the highest-leverage authoring moves for the migration thesis: each one adds a second (or foundational) card to a country that currently holds a lone date, turning it into a *held making tenure*.

- **South Korea (3 scattered cards → a continuous heavy-making arc).** Movable type 1377, Mega-Block shipbuilding 2002, 3D V-NAND 2013 read as three unconnected turns. Cards #1 (DRAM 1992), #2 (AMOLED 2007), #3 (POSCO 1973) and #12 (Li-ion cell ~2010) connect them into steel→ships→memory→displays→batteries — the corpus's cleanest modern "leadership migrated here and stayed" sequence.
- **Japan's modern CNC (single date → 18-year hold).** Only the 1956 CNC controller exists; #9 (FANUC 1974) is the conversion. Similarly #19 (Nikon lens) deepens Japan's precision-optics turn, and #20 (Sony TR-55 1955) gives the flash/memory/display cards a rooted origin instead of starting mid-story.
- **Taiwan (one industry → two pillars).** All 3 cards are Hsinchu semiconductor and stop at packaging (CoWoS 2011). #11 (FinFET 2018) extends the chip tenure to the frontier node; #22 (notebook ODM) adds systems assembly as a second pillar.
- **Shenzhen (1 card → a making capital).** Blade Battery is the lone Shenzhen card. #5 (Foxconn 2007) makes Shenzhen legible as the world's densest electronics-assembly cluster — a task-flagged hole.
- **China battery relay (jump → end-of-relay).** The thread already has three China cards; #6 (CATL) names the actual world-leading cell maker, and #12 (Korea) inserted before them turns Japan→China from a jump into Japan→Korea→China.
- **China Therapeutics / Energy roots.** One making card each (Antibiotic Fermentation; and zero in Energy). #23 (bulk API/vitamin C) and #4 (solar-PV) give each a sustained upstream-making presence.

## 4. Already well-covered — do NOT over-author here

These threads are already deep and globally distributed; adding more (especially more US) cards would dilute rather than strengthen the thesis.

- **Seeing Smaller** (10 cards, 0% US) and **Materials Characterization** (15 cards, 7% US) are essentially complete European/global spines — phase-contrast, SEM, STED, Raman, XPS, FT-NMR, Mössbauer all landed in the resync. Leave them.
- **Precision Optics** (20 cards, 25% US) and **Chip Lithography** (21 cards, 48% US) are richly built with German/UK/Japanese/Dutch/Taiwanese making. The *only* Chip-Litho gaps worth filling are the deep supply-chain chokepoints already listed (#7, #8, #11, #14, #15, #16) — do not add more general litho-history cards.
- **Reaching Orbit** (11 cards, 27% US) already holds real non-US tenures: France (Ariane), Japan (H-II), China (Long March), India (PSLV), Russia (Rocket Equation). No shoring-up needed.
- **Software Abstraction** is well-covered *from 1948 onward* (18 cards); the only gaps are the pre-1948 British/German *origins* (#30–33, #38). Do not add more mid-century US computing cards — the imbalance is temporal, not a shortage of US material.
- **Materials/Therapeutics/Engineering Biology** European and Canadian making (Insulin, Salvarsan, Gamma Knife, Pasteur, antibody humanization/phage display, Golden Rice, Siemens/Pidgeon/Verneuil processes) all landed. The single genuine addition worth making in these areas is the Chinese upstream-manufacturing layer (#23), not more invention cards.
